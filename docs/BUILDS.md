# Preset builds (the Build Master)

The **Build Master** stands by the Prontera fountain; `@build` opens it anywhere. New characters see it on their first login. It can:

- **apply a preset build**: stats, skills, gear, cards and refines for a tested build of your class, in one step. Only presets of the current episode are offered, and only with gear that exists in that episode.
- **build your own**: pick stats, skills and gear yourself.
- **remove a build** it gave you. It only ever takes back exactly what it handed out, never your own loot.

Preset builds follow the point rule: a build never gives more stat or skill points than your levels allow.
