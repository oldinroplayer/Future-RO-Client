# Episodes: what the world has, and when

Future RO plays Ragnarok's history one **episode** at a time. Every player is in the same episode, starting with **Episode 1.0 (August 2002)**. When the world moves on to the next episode, what kRO released then opens up: towns, fields and dungeons, monsters, classes and skills, higher level caps, and the rules of that time.

The game shows the current episode when you log in and on the Future RO website ("Now playing"). The World Warper, `@go` and `@warp` only reach maps the episode has.

## What happens to your character when the episode changes

- **Nothing is taken away.** Items, zeny and characters stay. Maps from later episodes are simply closed until their time; if you stand on one, you are sent to your save point.
- **Level caps follow the episode.** Levels above the current cap are kept safe and come back when the cap rises (your level and experience are remembered).
- **A class from a later episode** (only possible if the world went back in time) plays as the closest older class until its episode returns.
- **Renewal (Episode 13.2 "Renewal", 2009)** changed the formulas. Crossing that line in either direction **resets your stats and skills once** on your next login, so you can spend the points again. The Build Master (`@build`) can set up a preset build in one step.
- **Stat and skill points always match your levels.** You never have more free points than your levels give you; the server keeps that exact.
